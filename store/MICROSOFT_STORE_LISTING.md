## Descriptions

**Short Description:**
Turn PDFs into MP3 audiobooks in a few clicks.

**Long Description:**
SimpleAudioBookGen is a streamlined Windows application that converts your PDF documents into high-quality MP3 audiobooks. Designed for simplicity and efficiency, it provides a guided workflow to turn reading material into listening material without complex setups.

Whether you have a text-based research paper or a scanned document, SimpleAudioBookGen offers multiple voice engines—including state-of-the-art natural online voices and privacy-focused offline models—to suit your needs.

**Key Features:**
- **Easy Conversion:** Convert any PDF to MP3 with a simple, step-by-step interface.
- **Natural AI Voices:** Use high-quality neural voices for a human-like listening experience.
- **Offline Support:** Generate audiobooks locally without an internet connection using Kokoro or Piper engines.
- **OCR Integration:** Extract text from scanned PDFs using Tesseract OCR.
- **Voice Preview:** Listen to voice samples before starting the full conversion.
- **Customizable Quality:** Choose your preferred bitrate and audio settings.
- **Privacy First:** Your documents stay on your machine unless you choose the online voice mode.
