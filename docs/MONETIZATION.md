# Monetization Strategy

SimpleAudioBookGen follows a simple, transparent monetization model designed for a high-value, low-friction user experience.

## Initial Model: Paid App
For the initial release (v1.0), SimpleAudioBookGen will be a **paid application** on the Microsoft Store.

- **Type:** One-time purchase.
- **No Subscriptions:** Users pay once and own the version forever.
- **No In-App Purchases (v1):** All core features are included in the initial price.

## Pricing Recommendations

### Target Price: $0.99 USD / €0.99 EUR
*   **Reasoning:** This is the standard "low-tier" entry price for the Microsoft Store. It covers store fees and provides a small contribution to the developer while remaining an "impulse buy" for users.
*   **Perceived Value:** Extremely low pricing (under $0.99) can sometimes lead to a perception of "low quality" or "abandonware." $0.99 strikes a balance.

### Micro-Pricing Investigation: $0.25 USD
*   **Status:** Microsoft Partner Center occasionally allows lower tiers in specific regions or through "Sales," but $0.99 is the most consistent minimum tier across all markets.
*   **Recommendation:** Use **$0.99** as the base price. If a lower tier like $0.25 is available and stable, it could be used for a "Launch Sale" to boost initial rankings.

## Future Monetization Options
*   **Pro Version:** A separate version with advanced features (batch processing, advanced NLP cleaning).
*   **In-App Purchases:** For premium offline voice models or cloud-based OCR if implemented.
*   **Licensing for Business:** Volume licensing for educational institutions or companies.

## Store Fees
Microsoft typically takes a **15% commission** for non-gaming apps (please verify current Partner Center terms at the time of submission).
- At $0.99, the estimated payout per unit is ~$0.84 (excluding taxes).
